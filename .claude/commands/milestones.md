---
description: Milestones volunteers have reached that nobody has marked: 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and 2,500 hours, and 1, 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 years. Records the recognition.
---

1. Run `node scripts/volunteers.mjs milestones --json`.
2. List each person, the milestone and their hours to date. The biggest milestone first.
3. Offer `/draft-thank-you` for each, and `npm run docs -- recognition-certificate` once recognised.
4. Record it: `recognise "<who>" --milestone="10 years" --how="Morning tea and a card"`.
