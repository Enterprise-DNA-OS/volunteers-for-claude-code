---
description: Draft a note asking a volunteer to renew a Working with Children check, police check or licence that is expiring or expired. Drafts only.
---

1. Run `node scripts/volunteers.mjs volunteer "<who>" --json`. Read the check, the date it stops counting and any shifts after that date.
2. Never draft for a barred or pending outcome. Stop and say why.
3. Write `drafts/check-renewal-<ref>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`: which check, when it runs out, the shifts it affects, how to renew (the volunteer applies with the issuer in their state and sends the new number), and that the organisation will verify it once it arrives.
4. Short, practical, thankful. A person reads and sends.
