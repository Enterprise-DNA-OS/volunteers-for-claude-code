---
description: One volunteer's whole card before any call, roster change or letter: contact, checks, training, upcoming shifts, hours by program, milestones and notes.
---

1. Run `node scripts/volunteers.mjs volunteer "<name or ref>" --json`. A partial name works; if several match, show the list and ask which.
2. Lead with anything that blocks them: NOT CLEARED for a rostered shift, an expired or unverified check, no guardian consent under 18, no emergency contact.
3. Then the person: how long they have volunteered, hours in the last year, where they work, what is coming up, the last note.
4. If a milestone is due, say so and offer `/draft-thank-you`.
