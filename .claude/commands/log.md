---
description: Record a call, email, meeting or note against a volunteer.
---

1. Run `node scripts/volunteers.mjs log "<who>" --kind=call|email|meeting|note --body="<what happened>" [--by="<coordinator>" --on=<date>]`.
2. Write the note the way a coordinator would read it in six months: who, what they said, what happens next.
3. If the note changes something on the record (a new phone number, a break, a training date), make that change too with the matching command, and say so.
