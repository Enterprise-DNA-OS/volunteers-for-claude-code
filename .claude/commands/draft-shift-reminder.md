---
description: Draft a shift reminder or a request to fill a gap, to one volunteer or a short list. Drafts to drafts/; a person sends.
---

1. Run `node scripts/volunteers.mjs shifts --json` and `node scripts/volunteers.mjs volunteer "<who>" --json` for each person.
2. Skip anyone not cleared for the role on that day, and say why.
3. Write `drafts/shift-<shift ref>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`: one short message per person, first name, the role, the day, start and finish time, the place, who to ask for, and how to say yes or no. For a gap, say plainly how many are still needed.
4. Plain and warm. No pressure, no guilt. A person reads and sends.
