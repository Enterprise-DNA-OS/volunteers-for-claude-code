---
description: Draft a note to a volunteer who has quietly stopped coming. Drafts only.
---

1. Run `node scripts/volunteers.mjs volunteer "<who>" --json` and read the last notes.
2. If a note explains the absence (injury, illness, family), write a check-in, not an invitation to a shift.
3. Write `drafts/welcome-back-<ref>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`: thank them for what they have given (hours, how long, the role), say they are missed, name one or two shifts coming up in their usual role and day, and make it easy to say not now.
4. Never guilt. A person reads and sends.
